Terms and Conditions for Guaranteed Placement

To qualify for the 100% placement guarantee, participants must meet the following criteria:

  • Complete all assigned training modules, projects, and assessments with full participation.
  • Achieve a minimum score of 80% in the final evaluation exam conducted at the end of the program.
  • Attend all mock interviews, resume building sessions, and actual job interviews arranged by EmployFirst.
  • Maintain a professional demeanor and adhere to the code of conduct during the program and placement process.

Failure to meet any of these requirements may result in the forfeiture of the placement guarantee. In such cases, EmployFirst may offer additional support or partial refunds at its sole discretion. The guarantee applies only to placements in relevant tech roles with a minimum salary threshold as specified in the program agreement. EmployFirst does not guarantee specific companies, locations, or salary levels beyond the minimum. All decisions regarding placements and guarantees are final and binding.

By enrolling in the program, you agree to these terms and conditions.
